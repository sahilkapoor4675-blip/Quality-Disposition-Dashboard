# Deliberate minor-version bump (was ~=3.8.0): 3.8.x's tight-bbox handling for a
# twinx() dual-axis chart (used by the Pareto defect chart) could recurse deep
# enough with a broad/unfiltered report's defect list to blow Python's stack
# with a RecursionError during export. Stress-tested on 3.10.x up to 3,000
# chart labels and 400 repeated chart renders in one process with no issue.
# Combined with dropping the redundant bbox_inches="tight" in reports.py (which
# was the actual recursion trigger on top of fig.tight_layout()), this removes
# the failure rather than just raising the recursion ceiling further.
